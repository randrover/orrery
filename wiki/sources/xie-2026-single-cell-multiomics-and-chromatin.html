Single-cell multiomics and chromatin structure reveal gene-regulatory dynamics in heart failure

One-line: A cell type-resolved multimodal atlas of 36 human hearts (transcriptome, chromatin-accessibility, histone marks, and single-cell Hi-C genome-architecture) showing that heart failure coordinately rewires cis-regulatory-elements and higher-order genome organization, and links genome-wide-association-study risk variants to cell type-specific target genes.

Key takeaways

  • Built a unified map of 759,222 nuclei from 13 non-failing and 23 failing (13 ICM, 10 NICM) human hearts across all four chambers, combining 10x Multiome (ATAC+RNA), Droplet Paired-Tag (H3K27ac/H3K27me3 + RNA), and Droplet Hi-C, resolving 12 major cardiac cell types and 65 subpopulations. A pooled/multiplexed sampling strategy (subjects demultiplexed by genotype) minimized batch effects without degrading data quality.
  • Integrating the epigenomic layers annotated 285,873 candidate cis-regulatory-elements (cCREs; 17.4% previously uncharacterized vs ENCODE SCREEN), grouped by nNMF into 11 cell type-associated modules; a five-state ChromHMM model (active/open/weak/repressive/unclassified) captured lineage-specific regulation. ABC modeling (using Hi-C contacts + H3K27ac) linked distal cCREs to genes, yielding 66,317 cCRE-promoter pairs including 11,938 cell type-specific ones.
  • Heart failure drove widespread remodeling: DESeq2 found 10,400 differentially expressed genes and 52,738 differentially accessible cCREs (mostly distal), concentrated in cardiomyocytes, fibroblasts, and myeloid cells. Up-regulated cCREs shifted open-to-active; shared stress-response AP-1 motifs (JUN/FOS/ATF) activated broadly, while 37,924 regions lost H3K27me3, indicating global PRC2 derepression.
  • Gene-regulatory networks (built by integrating scRNA + scATAC) identified 41 cell type-specific and 23 shared disease-associated TFs; RNA-velocity trajectories revealed intermediate cardiomyocyte and fibroblast states (e.g. stress-responsive vCM4/vCM5, transitory FB4) bridging healthy and diseased conditions, marked by JUN/FOS/ATF/EGR/SMAD activity.
  • Single-cell Hi-C exposed cell type-specific 3D reorganization in disease: 5,221 bins underwent A/B compartment switching, and cardiomyocytes/fibroblasts showed increased A-to-B switching, reduced A-A interactions, decreased insulation, and gained long-range interactions within inactive B compartments (e.g. PRELID2 A-to-B down, DIAPH3 B-to-A up) — loss of active chromatin organization coupled to altered gene expression.
  • LD-score regression showed HF/DCM/arrhythmia risk variants enriched in cardiomyocyte-specific active cCREs, while hypertension/blood-pressure/MI variants mapped to fibroblast and smooth-muscle cCREs (distinguishing NICM- vs ICM-like mechanisms). 1,293 fine-mapped DCM variants (77.5% of loci) fell in cardiac cCREs; risk variants were most enriched in the stress-responsive intermediate vCM5 GRN.
  • Combining chromatin-loops (Hi-C), ABC, and ChromBPNet variant-effect predictions reassigned target genes for many DCM loci (22/33 differing from prior fine-mapping), confirmed known links (rs17099139-BAG3, rs754920-FLNC) and nominated new ones — e.g. rs13277721 (8q24) acting on TRAPPC9 via long-range looping by disrupting an AP-2α motif.

Methods / data

  • Human cardiac tissue; single-nucleus 10x Multiome, Droplet Paired-Tag, Droplet Hi-C; WGS for genotype demultiplexing and pathogenic-variant calling (LDLR, FLNC). Analyses: iterative peak calling + nNMF cCRE modules, ChromHMM chromatin states, ABC enhancer-gene links, SCENIC+ GRNs, scVelo trajectories, DESeq2 differential tests, LDSC partitioned heritability, fine-mapping, and ChromBPNet (variant-effect-prediction) for accessibility-altering variants.
  • Data browser resource released at epigenome.wustl.edu/HeartEpigenome/.

Why it matters / relevance

  • Extends single-cell 3D-genome mapping (chromatin-conformation-capture, see wen-2024-single-cell-multiplex-chromatin) from atlas-building to a disease context, showing that pathologic gene regulation involves coordinated disruption of both cis-regulatory-elements and higher-order chromatin-compartments / topologically-associating-domains.
  • Provides a mechanistic bridge from noncoding GWAS risk (noncoding-variants) to cell type-specific target genes via long-range chromatin-loops, offering a template for cell type-targeted therapeutic discovery in heart failure and complex disease more broadly.
